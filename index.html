02 — Making the jump

switching shouldn’t feel like a gamble.

Most people we talk to don’t stay with their current provider because they’re happy. They stay because changing sounds risky — the site might go down, nobody knows the passwords, and the invoices are already paid. Here’s exactly how we remove each of those.

  1. 01

    nothing goes dark

    Your existing site stays live and untouched the entire time we build. We only flip the switch when you’ve clicked through the new one and told us you’re happy. If the changeover ever went wrong, we point the domain back the way it was — same day.

  2. 02

    you control everything, from day one

    Domain, hosting, analytics, ad accounts, email — registered in your business’s name with your details, not ours, and you hold the logins. Nothing about how your business runs sits behind a door only we can open.

  3. 03

    we do the awkward admin

    We’ll read your current contract, tell you plainly what it says, and handle the transfer requests and notice period with your existing provider. You don’t have to have the uncomfortable phone call.

  4. 04

    no lock-in, ever

    Everything monthly runs month to month. Thirty days’ notice and we hand over a clean export of the lot — site, artwork, ad accounts — documented, to whoever comes next. We’d rather earn the next month than trap you in it.

  5. 05

    a fixed number, before we start

    You approve a written quote with a scope and a date on it. If we got the estimate wrong, that’s our problem, not a change-request invoice.

  6. 06

    actually local

    We can come and sit in your shop, office or unit, or stand on the job site and look at where the sign is going. Hard to sell a business you’ve never stood inside.

    06 — Questions we get asked

    the awkward ones, answered.

    do i have to take more than one thing?

    No. Every line is available on its own and you can buy a set of lawn signs from us and nothing else. The discount exists because running your website genuinely makes the rest cheaper for us to do — it isn't a penalty for saying no to the other bits.

    what does a website actually cost?

    It depends on how much of it there is, so we won't insult you with a number before we've spoken. What we will do is give you one fixed price up front, with the scope written down, so there's no drift. Most local businesses want something in the same shape — a handful of pages, a way to be contacted or booked, and it to be findable — and we'll tell you honestly if you need less than you think.

    how long does it take?

    A straightforward site is usually a few weeks from the go-ahead, and most of that is waiting on words and photos rather than on us. Signs depend on the printer's queue. Ads can be live in days, but give them a month before judging the numbers. Either way, the date goes in the quote.

    my current guy built our site. won't moving break everything?

    It's the right thing to worry about, and it's why we build the new site completely separately while your current one carries on. Nothing changes for your customers until you've seen the new one and said go. The domain move itself is minutes, and it's reversible.

    who owns the ad accounts and the artwork?

    You do, all of it. Ad accounts go in your business's name with you as owner, and you get the print-ready files for every sign, not just a PDF proof. If you leave, you leave with the lot — we're not holding your logo hostage to keep you.

    do we have to be in london to work with you?

    No, but it's who we're built for. Being able to drive over, stand behind the counter or look at the lot the sign is going on is the thing that makes this worth paying for rather than hiring whoever is cheapest online.

    we're mid-contract. is it worth talking now?

    Yes — that's exactly the situation the contract buyout is for. Send us what you're on and we'll work out what's left to run and what we'd knock off. Plenty of people find they're closer to the end than they thought.
